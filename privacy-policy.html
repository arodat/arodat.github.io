Privacy Policy — Container Cargo Capacity Calculator (CCCC)

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Overview

Container Cargo Capacity Calculator ("CCCC", "CBM Calculator", "the app") is a mobile utility for planning container cargo placement, calculating CBM / chargeable weight and validating container numbers. The app works offline and does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

All data you enter (container dimensions, cargo lists, saved plans, custom container types, unit and language preferences) is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server. Deleting the app deletes this data.

The app requests access to your photo library only when you choose to export a loading plan image, and uses it solely to save that image.

The app periodically fetches a small version-configuration file from our website to check whether a mandatory update is required. This request does not include any personal data.

Feedback you send us

If you use the in-app Feedback form, the subject and message you type are sent to us through the Web3Forms form service and delivered to our email inbox. Sending feedback is optional. Do not include personal information you do not want us to receive. We use these messages only to answer you and improve the app.

Advertising (free tier)

The free tier shows banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising ID / IDFA, where you have given permission)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)
  • Diagnostic and performance data (such as crash logs)

How this data is processed is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (PRO)

Purchases are processed by Google Play (or the App Store). We do not receive or store your payment details. To verify your PRO status, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device and your purchase history for this app.

Your choices

  • EEA / UK users: on first launch you are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can refuse or customize consent; the app remains fully functional and will show non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the app.
  • iOS users: you can decline tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; ads will then be non-personalized.
  • You can reset or delete your device advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

Purchasing PRO removes advertising entirely.

Data deletion

The app has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. You can delete your data as follows:

  • Data on your device (plans, cargo lists, settings): uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings. This deletes it immediately and permanently.
  • Feedback messages and purchase records: send us a deletion request by email (birgulayhankovan@gmail.com) with the subject "CCCC data deletion request". For purchase records, include your Google Play order number (GPA.xxxx). We delete the feedback messages and ask RevenueCat to delete the associated customer record within 30 days. Google Play may keep transaction records it is legally required to keep.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Data held by Google can be managed through your Google account.

Children

The app is a professional logistics tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob for advertising, RevenueCat for purchase verification, Web3Forms for feedback delivery), for the purposes described.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
