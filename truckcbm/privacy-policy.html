Privacy Policy — Truck CBM Calculator

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Application ID: com.biayko.truckcbm

Overview

Truck CBM Calculator ("the app") is a mobile utility for planning truck and semi-trailer loads: it calculates loading metres (LDM), volume (CBM) and a top-down load plan. The app works offline and does not require an account. It is free and funded by advertising.

Data we collect

None. Everything you enter — vehicle selection, your own vehicles, pallet rows, dimensions, weights, saved plans, unit preference, language and theme — is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server of ours. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and we do not have a user database, so there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete on request. The only third-party SDKs in the app are the advertising and purchase SDKs described below; the data they collect is collected and processed by those providers, not by us.

Network requests the app makes

  • Version check. The app fetches a small JSON configuration file from our developer site (hosted on GitHub Pages) to see whether a mandatory update is required. The request carries no personal data; like any web request, the hosting provider receives your IP address.
  • Feedback. If you use the feedback form, only the subject and the message you type are sent to us, through the form-delivery service Web3Forms, so we can read them. Nothing else is attached and no contact details are asked for. If the form is unavailable, the app opens your own mail app instead; nothing is sent until you send that e-mail yourself, and if you do, we receive it like any e-mail, including your e-mail address.
  • Advertising and purchases. See the two sections below.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the advertising ID (on iOS the IDFA, only where you allow tracking)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App activity related to ads, such as ad views and clicks
  • Diagnostics about ad delivery and performance

How Google processes this is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Where an ad-free option is offered and you buy it, the purchase is processed by Google Play and validated through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and the receipt in order to confirm your entitlement. We never see or store your payment details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • EEA / UK users: you are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before the ad SDK is initialised. You can refuse or customise consent; the app remains fully functional and will show non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy options" at the bottom of the Plan screen.
  • iOS users: you can decline tracking at the App Tracking Transparency prompt; ads will then be non-personalised.
  • You can reset or delete your device advertising ID in your device settings at any time.
  • Where offered, buying the ad-free option removes advertising entirely.

Permissions

This version of the app requests no access to your photos, camera, location, contacts or microphone. If a future version needs one, for example to save a load plan as an image, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

The app is a professional logistics tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share any with third parties ourselves. The advertising and purchase data described above is collected and processed directly by Google and RevenueCat under their own policies.

Not a legal or safety instrument

The load distribution figures in the app are an estimate calculated from the plan you entered and from typical data for the vehicle class — not from your vehicle. They are not a legal weighing and are not a statement of road-legal compliance. Only a weighbridge shows your real axle loads. This paragraph is here because it concerns how you should rely on the app, not because it concerns your data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
